Research

Higher-order clustering statistics of the large-scale structure

My work develops and applies joint analyses of the two- and three-point correlation functions (2PCF + 3PCF) of galaxy clustering, aiming to extract cosmological information beyond what standard two-point statistics can capture. This includes analytical modelling of the three-point correlation function in the mildly nonlinear regime, and its first joint application together with the 2PCF to real survey data.

Within the Euclid Consortium, I contributed the full-shape real-space modelling of the 2PCF and 3PCF used in the Euclid preparation papers, and led the first joint 2PCF+3PCF full-shape analysis of real spectroscopic data (BOSS DR12), currently under peer review.

Massimo Guidi
